About Aureva

We believe yoga should be reachable on any given day — in a body that is tired, stiff or brand new to it. Through patient guidance and classes built around real people, we help members steady their breath, soften what is held, and carry that ease home with them.

Six ways to practise with us. Every class is capped at fourteen mats, so your guide can actually see you.

  • Beginner yoga

    Foundations, one pose at a time. No experience, no flexibility and no special kit needed.

    Mon & Thu · 60 min

  • Meditation

    Guided sittings that teach you to notice a thought without following it.

    Daily · 30 min

  • Stress relief

    Slow, supported shapes held for minutes at a time to let the nervous system drop down a gear.

    Wed & Sun · 75 min

  • Flexibility & mobility

    Hips, hamstrings and shoulders — practical range you will feel at your desk by Wednesday.

    Tue & Fri · 60 min

  • Mindfulness

    Breathwork and attention training you can use on a commute, not only on a mat.

    Sat morning · 45 min

  • Personal sessions

    One-to-one time built around an injury, a pregnancy or simply your own pace.

    By appointment · 50 min
